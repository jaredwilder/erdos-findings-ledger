# erdos-findings-ledger

**52 verdict files**, one per mathematical result, each recording what was found, what it means,
and what it does not mean. Selected from a 254-file ledger by excluding every entry touching
clinical or biological work.

Author: Jared Wilder. First public timestamp: 2026-09-10.

## What a finding file is

Not a paper and not a proof. A verdict: the claim, the evidence path, the verification method, and
the ceiling on what may be concluded. The ledger exists so a result cannot quietly grow between
the day it was computed and the day it is cited.

## What is in the 52

Sidon set sizes across a ladder from n = 128 to n = 1000, and the divergence at n = 35 whose
novelty is recorded as **unresolved** because the exact constraint variant was never written down.
Circulant Ramsey family elimination. An R(5,5) lower certificate and obstruction. Covering siege
results. The Erdos 203 covering refutation, closed. The Erdos 411 r=2 totient reduction. Erdos 902
Schutte tournament bounds. Erdos 595's barrier package. Erdos 710's autonomous close. The certified
enumeration of planar integral point sets.

## The ones worth reading first are the ones that went wrong

A finding recording that a **benchmark citation was fabricated**: a script claimed a specific
AlphaProof result with two numbers that were invented, caught and retracted by the author.

A finding recording that the **Erdos 950 growth mechanism was refuted** by its own data, with the
oscillation band and the R-squared near zero.

A finding recording that an **auto-scope generalization made results worse, not better**, kept as a
validated negative rather than deleted.

Those three are why the other 49 are worth anything.

## Scope

These are verdicts pointing at evidence, much of which is published in the companion repositories.
A verdict is not independent verification. Where a finding says CLOSED, the artifact it points to
is the thing to check, not the verdict line.
