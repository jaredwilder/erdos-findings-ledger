# erdos-findings-ledger

**52 scope-pinned mathematical verdicts** covering Sidon bounds, Ramsey structure, covering designs,
Erdős reductions, tournament bounds, barrier theorems, and certified integral-point-set
computations. Each verdict records the claim, evidence path, verification method, and exact ceiling
on what the evidence establishes.

Selected from a 254-file findings ledger after excluding every entry touching clinical or
biological work.

Author: Jared Wilder. First public timestamp: 2026-09-10.

## What is in the 52

Sidon set sizes across a ladder from n = 128 to n = 1000, and the divergence at n = 35 whose
novelty is recorded as **unresolved** because the exact constraint variant was never written down.
Circulant Ramsey family elimination. An R(5,5) lower certificate and obstruction. Covering siege
results. The Erdős 203 covering refutation, closed. The Erdős 411 r=2 totient reduction. Erdős 902
Schütte tournament bounds. Erdős 595's barrier package. Erdős 710's autonomous close. The certified
enumeration of planar integral point sets.

## What a finding file records

A finding is a compact research verdict: the mathematical claim, the evidence path, the
verification method, and the ceiling on what may be concluded. The ledger exists so a result cannot
quietly grow between the day it was computed and the day it is cited.

## Integrity controls that survived publication

The ledger also preserves the cases where its own process caught a bad result or bad provenance:

- a **fabricated benchmark citation**: a script claimed a specific AlphaProof result with two
  invented numbers; it was caught and retracted by the author;
- an **Erdős 950 growth mechanism refuted by its own data**, with the oscillation band and the
  R-squared near zero;
- an **auto-scope generalization that made results worse, not better**, retained as a validated
  negative rather than deleted.

Those records are evidence that the claim ceilings are enforced; they are not the headline of the
other mathematical results.

## Scope

These verdicts point at evidence, much of which is published in companion repositories. A verdict
is not independent verification. Where a finding says CLOSED, the artifact it points to is the
thing to check, not the verdict line.
